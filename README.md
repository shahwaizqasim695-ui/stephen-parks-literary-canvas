# Literary Canvas Studio

Act as a senior UI/UX designer and frontend engineer.

I am providing the official full book cover for:

Title: Lust and Love and the Difference Of
Author: Stephen Parks

I want you to design and build a premium, professional, cinematic author/book landing website inspired directly by the visual identity of the provided book cover.

TECH STACK
- React
- Vite
- JavaScript or TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS preferred
- Framer Motion for animations
- Lucide React for icons
- Clean, modular, reusable components
- Production-quality folder structure
- Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Optimized for SEO, accessibility, and performance

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT make this look like a generic SaaS template.
Do NOT use random stock photography.
Do NOT introduce bright modern colors that conflict with the cover.
The entire website should feel like an extension of the book itself.

--------------------------------------------------
DESIGN DIRECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Study the attached book cover carefully and extract its visual language.

The cover uses:
- near-black / charcoal textured surfaces
- aged ivory / parchment tones
- distressed cream typography
- hand-drawn black-and-white illustrations
- vintage ornamental borders
- subtle chain motifs
- old literary / romantic / academic atmosphere
- dramatic contrast
- handcrafted print texture

Translate that aesthetic into a sophisticated modern website.

The website should feel:

Premium
Literary
Editorial
Romantic
Mysterious
Atmospheric
Handcrafted
Mature
Cinematic

It should NOT feel:
- childish
- cartoonish
- overly gothic
- horror-themed
- corporate
- SaaS-like
- overly animated
- cluttered

Use a refined balance between modern UI structure and vintage book-design aesthetics.

--------------------------------------------------
COLOR PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------

Base the palette on the cover.

Suggested colors:

Background:
#0C0C0B
#11110F
#171612

Primary text:
#E8E0CF
#F2EBDD

#B8AE9B

Border / decorative lines:
#776F61
#9A907E

Avoid pure white wherever possible.

Use slightly warm off-white text instead.

No vibrant blues, reds, purples, gradients, or modern neon colors.

--------------------------------------------------
TYPOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Typography is extremely important.

Headings:
Use an expressive literary serif or distressed editorial serif inspired by the title lettering.

Examples of suitable Google Font directions:
- Cormorant Garamond
- EB Garamond
- Libre Baskerville
- Cinzel Decorative only for very small ornamental accents
- Playfair Display

Body:
Use a very readable serif or restrained sans-serif.

Possible combination:
Headings: Cormorant Garamond
Body: Inter or Source Sans 3

Avoid making all text distressed.
Only major headings can have an expressive literary feeling.

Maintain excellent readability.

--------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL VISUAL LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------

Create:
- subtle paper grain
- distressed edge textures
- faint ornamental line work
- thin vintage borders
- occasional chain-inspired decorative lines
- tiny hand-drawn ornament separators
- soft vignette
- layered parchment cards
- subtle ink-style dividers

Do not overuse decoration.

The premium feel should come from:
space,
typography,
composition,
texture,
and restraint.

--------------------------------------------------
PAGE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------

Build the website as a polished single-page book landing website.

SECTIONS:

1. NAVIGATION
2. HERO
3. ABOUT THE BOOK
4. THE STORY / PREMISE
5. THEMES
6. CHARACTER / RELATIONSHIP SECTION
7. FEATURED QUOTE
8. BOOK DETAILS
9. AUTHOR SECTION
10. PURCHASE CTA
11. FOOTER

--------------------------------------------------
1. NAVIGATION
--------------------------------------------------

Create an elegant transparent navigation over the hero.

Desktop:
Left:
small book/author wordmark

Center/right navigation:
- The Story
- Themes
- About the Author
- Book Details

Right:
BUY THE BOOK button

The CTA should use an ivory outline and subtle hover fill.

Navigation becomes slightly darker / blurred when scrolling.

Mobile:
minimal hamburger menu.

Keep it premium and clean.

--------------------------------------------------
2. HERO SECTION
--------------------------------------------------

The hero must immediately communicate the book.

Use a cinematic split-screen composition.

LEFT SIDE:
- small eyebrow: A NOVEL BY STEPHEN PARKS
- large title:
  Lust
  and
  Love
  and the
  Difference Of

Do not force the full title onto one line.

Under it include the hook:

"Some places become memories.
Blissview University becomes home."

Then add a short supporting description based on the back cover:

At Blissview University, Sophia discovers friendship, belonging, and a kind of family she never expected. But when her bond with Jonathan Thomas grows into something neither can ignore, love and duty begin to collide.

Buttons:

Primary:
BUY THE BOOK

Secondary:
DISCOVER THE STORY

RIGHT SIDE:
Display the provided FRONT BOOK COVER prominently.

Prefer a premium book mockup / flat cover presentation with:
- slight 3D perspective
- subtle warm shadow
- no cheap floating animation
- no glossy commercial effect

Behind the cover, use abstract shapes inspired by the original hand-drawn artwork and parchment texture.

Add subtle slow parallax.

The hero should take approximately 90–100vh on desktop.

--------------------------------------------------
3. ABOUT THE BOOK
--------------------------------------------------

Make this section editorial and spacious.

Section label:
THE NOVEL

Headline:
"Some places become memories.
Blissview University becomes home."

Use a two-column layout.

Left:
large editorial typography / pull quote.

Right:
Book description:

"For Sophia, being at Blissview is more than a college education. Surrounded by loyal friends, inspiring mentors, and a campus unlike any other, she discovers that family isn't always something you're born into. Sometimes it's something you find.

But as Sophia's bond with Jonathan Thomas grows into something neither of them can ignore, they find themselves walking a path where love and duty collide."

Use tasteful decorative separators inspired by the book cover.

03

Cards should use:
- charcoal panels
- thin parchment outlines
- editorial numbering
- restrained hover animation

Do not use colorful icons.

--------------------------------------------------
6. SOPHIA & JONATHAN SECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Create a cinematic character-focused section inspired by the hand-drawn couple on the book cover.

Do not generate random realistic AI people.

Use either:
- cropped / isolated artwork from the supplied cover where appropriate,
OR
- abstract silhouettes / line-art placeholders that match the cover's illustration aesthetic.

Headline:

"SOME CONNECTIONS CHANGE THE COURSE OF EVERYTHING."

Text:

Sophia came to Blissview looking for an education.
What she found was belonging.

Jonathan represents something far more complicated—a relationship caught between affection, responsibility, and choices neither can avoid forever.

Use a bold editorial composition with the text overlapping or sitting beside an oversized monochrome illustration.

Sometimes it's something you find."

Add a tiny ornamental symbol above and below.

No unnecessary graphics.

--------------------------------------------------
ANIMATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Use Framer Motion.

Animations must be subtle and premium.

Recommended:
- fade-up on section entry
- staggered text reveal
- slight image parallax
- decorative line expansion
- slow book-cover floating movement of only a few pixels
- smooth button transitions
- subtle card elevation

Animation duration:
roughly 0.5–1 second.

No dramatic spinning.
No bouncing.
No excessive zooming.
No animation that harms accessibility.

Respect:
prefers-reduced-motion.

--------------------------------------------------
RESPONSIVE DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------

Desktop:
Strong editorial compositions and generous whitespace.

Tablet:
Simplify asymmetric layouts while retaining visual personality.

Mobile:
- stack content vertically
- maintain strong title hierarchy
- keep body text readable
- full-width primary CTA
- reduce decorative elements
- ensure book cover is visible without overwhelming viewport
- minimum 44px interactive touch targets

Avoid horizontal overflow under all circumstances.

--------------------------------------------------
ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------

Must include:
- semantic HTML5
- correct heading hierarchy
- keyboard accessible navigation
- visible focus styles
- sufficient contrast
- alt text for book artwork
- aria labels where appropriate
- reduced motion support
- accessible mobile menu

- typography
- composition
